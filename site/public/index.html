Your files, your photos and your house, on a machine at home.

Instead of somebody else’s servers. One sign-in for all of it, at your own address, from the browser or the TPCloud app.

/store · /photos

Files and photos

A file browser for everyone at home, the iPhone’s photos backed up in the background, and share links with or without a password.

/home

The house

Blinds and lights on a floor plan, with switches running tphome’s own firmware. And from the terminal: tphome --blind B0301 up.

/login

One sign-in

Passkeys on the web, password and two-factor in the app, and every session listed so you can close the ones you don’t know.

Three steps, on a Raspberry Pi or an old PC.

Run the command

On the machine itself: curl -fsSL https://tpcloud.migueltaibo.com/install | sh. It picks the file for that machine.

Answer a few questions

Where files go, whether you want the house, how you’ll reach it (only at home, Tailscale or your own domain) and your account.

Open it

From the browser or the app. On the server, tpcloud shows what’s running, accounts, modules and logs.
